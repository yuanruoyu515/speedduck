# speedduck artwork

The duck mascot was generated using the built-in imagegen tool, following the owner’s request for a white duck in the style of their installed CopyDuck reference, with blue fast-forward symbols. It is not a third-party endorsement. The original transparent generated image is `extension/icons/duck-original.png`; app and extension icons are resized exports.

## Generation prompt

Preserve the reference white duck facing right, thick rounded charcoal outline, oval eye, two-feather tuft, orange beak, pale peach cheek and plump body. Adapt only the wing into vivid blue double right-pointing fast-forward triangles. Clean flat vector-like rendering; transparent background, white body interior; balanced square composition with safe margins. One cutout logo. No text, shadows, background, accessories or extra ducks.
